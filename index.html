Grammar I: Yes / No questions with "Is there...?"

We use Is there... ? to ask if a single object exists in a specific room or place in our home.

Asking Questions

Is there a lamp in the bedroom?

Is there a TV in the kitchen?

Short Answers

Yes, there is.

No, there isn't. (isn't = is not)

Grammar II: Present Progressive (He / She is + verb-ing)

We use the Present Progressive to describe actions that are happening right now in our home.

Location & Actions

Where is your sister? — She's in the kitchen.

What's she doing? — She's eating.

Key Contractions

What's = What is

She's = She is / He's = He is

Basic Level Examples

Example 1: Identifying a Lamp

Question: Is there a lamp in the bedroom?

Resolution: Look at the bedroom image in the textbook. Yes, there is a lamp on the nightstand. Answer: Yes, there is.

Example 2: Identifying a TV in the kitchen

Question: Is there a TV in the kitchen?

Resolution: Look at the kitchen appliances. There is a stove and fridge, but no television. Answer: No, there isn't.

Example 3: Sister sleeping

Question: Where is the baby? What is she doing?

Resolution: The baby is in the crib inside the bedroom and resting. Answer: She's in the bedroom. She's sleeping.

Example 4: Brother in the bathroom

Question: Where is the brother? What is he doing?

Resolution: The boy is inside the bathtub filled with water. Answer: He's in the bathroom. He's taking a bath.

Example 5: Grandmother cooking

Question: Where is the grandmother? What is she doing?

Resolution: She stands by the stove stirring a pot. Answer: She's in the kitchen. She's cooking.

Intermediate Level Examples

Example 6: Constructing a question for a bed

Prompt: Complete the question: "___ a bed in the kitchen?"

Resolution: Beds belong in bedrooms, not kitchens. Structure requires "Is there". Answer: Is there a bed in the kitchen? No, there isn't.

Example 7: Father cleaning the living room

Question: Where is the father? What is he doing?

Resolution: He is holding a vacuum cleaner in the living room. Answer: He's in the living room. He's cleaning.

Example 8: Analyzing living room items

Question: Is there a TV in the living room?

Resolution: Check the living room layout in Our World 1 page 45. A television is mounted on the wall. Answer: Is there a TV in the living room? Yes, there is.

Example 9: Matching action to room

Prompt: She's watching TV. Where is she?

Resolution: Televisions are typically located in the living room. Answer: She's in the living room. She's watching TV.

Example 10: Bathrobe and bathroom check

Question: Is there a towel in the bathroom?

Resolution: Examine the bathroom illustration on page 45. A towel hangs near the sink. Answer: Yes, there is.

Advanced Level Examples

Example 11: Multi-room drawing description

Prompt: Draw a living room and write two sentences about it.

Resolution: Combine room identification with object existence. Answer: It's a living room. Is there a sofa in your picture? Yes, there is.

Example 12: Complex compound home query

Prompt: Formulate a negative existence check for a lamp in the bathroom and explain.

Resolution: Combine grammar rules: "Is there a lamp in the bathroom? No, there isn't. Lamps are in bedrooms or living rooms." Answer: Is there a lamp in the bathroom? No, there isn't.

Example 13: Multi-person action coordination

Prompt: Father is cleaning and grandmother is cooking. Where are they?

Resolution: Map each person to their respective household zone. Answer: Father is in the living room cleaning. Grandmother is in the kitchen cooking.

Example 14: Personalizing picture descriptions

Prompt: What is your picture about and is there a table?

Resolution: Answer custom room creation questions in 1st grade context. Answer: It's a dining room. Is there a table in your picture? Yes, there is.

Example 15: Full continuous tense dialogue construction

Prompt: Construct a question and answer for a grandmother in the kitchen using present progressive.

Resolution: Combine location question and action question. Answer: Where's the grandmother? She's in the kitchen. What's she doing? She's cooking.
